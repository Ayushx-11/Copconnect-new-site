CopBot's Safety Guide

Cyber Safety Tips for Everyone

Your friendly neighbourhood CopBot shares what every family needs to know.

For Kids & Teens

Your Digital Footprint Never Disappears

Everything you post leaves a trace. Ask yourself: would I be okay if Mum, Dad, or my teacher saw this?

"Once it's online, it's online forever. Think before you type!"
For Families

Parents & Kids: One Safety Team

Families that talk openly about online experiences stay far better protected. Try a weekly "digital check-in."

"When families stay connected, scammers stay out!"
For Parents

Know Your Child's Digital World

You wouldn't let a stranger walk into your home. Don't let them into your child's phone. Learn the apps they use.

"Awareness, not surveillance. Trust is your best parental control."
Password Safety

Your First Line of Defence

Use a different password for every account. Enable two-factor authentication everywhere. Use a password manager.

"123456 is not a password. Neither is your birthday!"
UPI & Finance

Never Share Your OTP — Ever

No bank, no UPI app, and no police officer will ever ask for your OTP or PIN on a call. If they do — hang up.

"Real cops never ask for your OTP. That's how you spot fraud."
Reporting Cyber Crime

Know How to Report — Act Fast

Been a victim? Call Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in. The sooner you report, the better.

"You are not alone. CopConnect walks with you every step."
