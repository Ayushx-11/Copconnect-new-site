About CopConnect

Protecting the World
from Cyber Crimes.

CopConnect is India's most human-centred cyber safety movement. We believe that cyber safety isn't a technology problem — it's a people problem. And people solve people problems together.

Backed by ISAC Foundation and a national network of Cyber Crime Intervention Officers, we operate at the intersection of community, trust, and action. Every session we run, every CCIO we train — it points to one goal: a safer India.

CopBot's Safety Guide

Cyber Safety Tips for Everyone

Your friendly neighbourhood CopBot shares what every family needs to know to stay safe online.

For Kids & Teens

Your Digital Footprint Never Disappears

Everything you post, share, or send leaves a trace. Ask yourself: would I be okay if Mum, Dad, or my teacher saw this?

"Once it's online, it's online forever. Think before you type!"
For Families Together

Parents & Kids: One Safety Team

Families that talk openly about online experiences are far better protected. Set a weekly "digital check-in" at home.

"When families stay connected, scammers stay out!"
For Parents

Know Your Child's Digital World

You wouldn't let a stranger walk into your home. Don't let them into your child's phone. Learn the apps they use.

"Awareness, not surveillance. Trust is your best parental control."
Password Safety

Passwords: Your First Line of Defence

Use a different password for every account. Enable two-factor authentication everywhere. Use a password manager.

"123456 is not a password. Neither is your birthday!"
UPI & Finance

Never Share Your OTP — Ever

No bank, no UPI app, and no police officer will ever ask for your OTP or PIN on a call. If they do — hang up immediately.

"Real cops never ask for your OTP. That's how you spot fraud."
Reporting Cyber Crime

Know How to Report — Act Fast

Been a victim? Call Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in. The sooner you report, the better.

"You are not alone. CopConnect walks with you every step."
